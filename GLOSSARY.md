# Cadence

Cadence sells video courses to learners. This glossary covers the language around getting money back after a purchase. These are the words we use everywhere, including in the emails and screens learners see.

## Language

### Refunds

**Refund Request**:
A learner's ask, with their reason, to get back what they paid for a purchase. The team approves or declines each Refund Request, and a purchase can have only one undecided Refund Request at a time and none after an approved one.
_Avoid_: Refund (for the ask itself), Cancellation, Reversal, Chargeback

**Open Refund Request**:
A Refund Request the team hasn't decided yet.
_Avoid_: Pending, Requested, Awaiting review

**Approved Refund Request**:
A Refund Request the team has accepted. Approving it is final: it leads to a Refund and ends the learner's access to the course.

**Declined Refund Request**:
A Refund Request the team has turned down. It has no Refund, and the learner may raise a new Refund Request.

**Refund**:
The money owed back to a learner once their Refund Request is approved: always the full amount they paid. It goes to the card they paid with unless a Failed Refund has to be sent another way.
_Avoid_: Reversal, Chargeback, Cancellation

**Pending Refund**:
A Refund the team is sending, either for the first time or again after it failed.
_Avoid_: Pending (on its own, which reads as a Refund Request awaiting review)

**Issued Refund**:
A Refund where the money has been sent back to the learner. It can still take a few days to reach them.
_Avoid_: Completed, Paid out

**Failed Refund**:
A Refund where the money couldn't be sent. The Refund Request stays approved and access stays ended, and the team retries it, to the same card or another way, as a Pending Refund again.
